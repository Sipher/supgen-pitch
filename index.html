In one 2026 study, a 7B model failed all 64 attempts on 595 math problems. Every one of those attempts was thrown away.

Why: in RL training (GRPO), when every attempt fails, all rewards are zero and the update is exactly zero. So labs filter these problems out (DAPO, Open-Reasoner-Zero). Source: Off-Context GRPO, arXiv 2607.19313.
